Atmospheric Thermodynamics

Interactive Skew-T Parcel Lab

Build a sounding, place a parcel, and follow its buoyant motion.

Available while the simulation is stopped or paused. Hold either button, Space, or Enter to move the parcel in the labeled direction. Releasing it sets vertical velocity to zero.
